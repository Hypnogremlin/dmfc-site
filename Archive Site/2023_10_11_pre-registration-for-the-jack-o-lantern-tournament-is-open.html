Pre-Registration for the Jack-O-Lantern Tournament is open!

It’s time to get excited for a truly TERRIFYING tourney! The 5th Annual Jack-O-Lantern Tournament is now open for registration! Whether you fence foil, saber, or epee we look forward to seeing all your smiling ghoulish faces this October! Be sure to register on AskFred here!
Details are below, and remember to reach out to coach Jason if you would like to volunteer for this exciting event!

LOCATION:

Vine Street Child Care

2501 Vine St

West Des Moines, IA 50265

There is plenty of parking in front of the building.

TOURNAMENT & EVENT FEES:

Pre-registration is $30

Each event $10

Day of Event registration fee +$5

Cash or checks, please. We do not accept credit at this time

ADDITIONAL DETAILS:

AskFred registration deadline October 20th, 2023 at Midnight (Pay day of event)

Medals for 1st, 2nd, two 3rd placements

USFA access or competitive membership will be required for all sanctioned events

Please bring proof of current membership in the USFA

All USFA rules will be enforced.

QUESTIONS:

Please contact us through AskFred with any questions related to the event.

TOURNAMENT CONTACT INFORMATION:

Event Coordinator: Jason Reis

Phone: (515) 303-0143‬

Email: DMFCcoaches@gmail.com
