date: 2026-07-18

---

DMFC showed up and showed out at this year's [Iowa Games](https://www.iowagames.org)
— the event's 40th anniversary. Huge congratulations to our fencers who
took home **medals in Senior Saber** and in both **Senior and Youth Foil**,
and to every club member who stepped on the strip. It was a great day for
the club — KCCI's cameras were even courtside to catch some of the action.
