We're excited to announce that **club membership is now live on this
website** — no more Google Form.

## What's changing

Every current member will soon receive an email inviting them to renew
their membership through our new online system. It's a straightforward
step up from the old Google Form: sign in with your email, fill out (or
confirm) your athlete info, emergency contacts, medical details, and
waivers, and you're done. Returning members can update their information
any time from their dashboard instead of filling out a form from scratch
every season.

**New members** joining the club going forward will use the exact same
process — there's no separate path anymore. Whether you're renewing or
joining for the first time, it all starts in one place:

[Sign in or create an account →](/login?next=/member/enroll)

## What to expect

- **Current members:** watch your inbox for a renewal email over the
  coming days. It will link you straight to the sign-in page.
- **New members:** use the link above any time — no need to wait for an
  invite.
- **Families:** one login can hold multiple athletes, so parents with
  more than one fencer in the club only need a single account.

If you run into any trouble signing in or renewing, reach out to
[DMFCPresident@gmail.com](mailto:DMFCPresident@gmail.com) and we'll get
you sorted out.

Thanks for being part of Des Moines Fencing Club — we think this new
system will make renewing (and joining) a lot easier for everyone.
