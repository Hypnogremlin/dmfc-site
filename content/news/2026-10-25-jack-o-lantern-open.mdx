## About the tournament

The Jack-O-Lantern Open is one of four annual tournaments hosted at the
Des Moines Fencing Club. Held the weekend before Halloween, it draws
fencers from across Iowa and the surrounding region for a day of
competition across all three weapons.

The event is open to USA Fencing members at all classification levels.
Beginners are welcome — there are typically Y10 and Y12 events with light
fields where newer fencers can get tournament experience in a low-pressure
setting.

## What to expect

- Three weapons run on parallel strips throughout the day
- Snacks and water provided; lunch available nearby
- Spectators welcome — no admission charge
